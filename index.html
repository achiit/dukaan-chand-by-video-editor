About

I started on a borrowed laptop.

In 2024 my cousin lent me his old laptop and I edited my first reel for a friend's mobile shop near Katihar station. He paid me ₹100 and posted it the same night. It got more views than anything he had ever put up.

That night I decided editing would be my work. Two years later I still edit every video myself. No agency, no outsourcing. When you message me, you talk to the person who will cut your footage.

I promise simple things: a fair price told upfront, delivery on the day I say, and changes until you are happy within the free revisions.

  • Price confirmed before work starts
  • You talk to the editor, not a manager
  • 2 free revisions on every job
  • Hindi, Urdu and English captions
  • Local taste: shaadi, Eid, Chhath, school events
